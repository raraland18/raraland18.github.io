I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Colorado State University, on the 2026&ndash;2027 academic job market. My research sits at the intersection of labor economics, health economics, and macroeconomics, examining how labor-market institutions, financial structure, and income distribution shape population health, and how the resulting health dynamics feed back into macroeconomic performance.

**Fields:** Macroeconomics, Labor Economics, Health Economics
**Topics:** Deaths of Despair &middot; Employment Protection Legislation &middot; Growth Cycles &middot; Financialization
